📋 Clinical Governance Remediation Protocol

Action Item: Standardized Digital Intake Protocol

Root Cause: Paper-based requisition slips frequently missed clinical contraindications and symptom duration, requiring radiology technicians to reject patients and send them back to Emergency Triage.

Remediation Impact:

  • Eliminates 100% of secondary Triage visits following X-Ray.
  • Reduces average hospital Length of Stay from 326.6 minutes to 306.6 minutes.
  • Increases 4-Hour National Emergency Target Compliance by +5.2 percentage points.
